Context and takeaways

Before week 1

From Oct 20 to Nov 6 the same paper account ran a very different, concentrated book (FICO, SNDK, LITE, including fractional shares on margin). Equity fell from about $36,000 on Oct 28 to $6,046.90 at the Nov 6 close, roughly -83%, and $210.03 of margin interest was charged on Nov 2. A $3,000 paper deposit on Nov 3 is inside that window. Week 1 of the daily test therefore started on Nov 9 from a flat book and a $6,046.91 base.

Since week 1

Since week 1 the cadence has been lighter: Nov 16 traded SPOT (stopped at breakeven, -$0.09) and TM (-$25.33); Nov 17 to 19 had no fills; Nov 20 bought INTU (19 sh), FICO (20 sh, closed +$404.60) and EPAM (closed +$23.59). INTU was still open when this report was pulled, showing -$316.47 unrealized against account equity of $9,368.67.

What the week showed

  1. Intraday round trips did the work: 19 same-day trades netted +$254.84 (10 winners), while the 4 overnight holds netted only +$19.80 with a wide spread (MRVL +$155.10 versus TEM -$124.00). Overnight exposure added variance, not edge.
  2. The win rate was 52% but the profit factor was 1.94 because the average winner ($47.23) was 1.8x the average loser ($26.55). The edge this week came from letting CEG, TER, OKLO and MRVL run, not from being right often.
  3. Sizing was a flat 10 shares (25 for OKLO, 50 for VSTM) regardless of price, so notional per trade ranged from $267 to $3,941. Risk per trade was uncontrolled; TSLA and AAPL at ~$3,500 to $3,900 notional were the two largest intraday losers.
  4. Every one of the 49 week-1 orders was entered by hand: 43 through the Alpaca web dashboard and 6 through an API key for the OCO brackets. This was a human-in-the-loop test of the signals, not an autonomous bot run, so execution timing (first fills 9:32 to 11:50 AM, exits clustered 2:48 to 3:51 PM) reflects the operator's schedule.
  5. OCO brackets were introduced only on Friday. All five stop legs that could trigger did trigger, at 1% to 2% below entry on EAT, which suggests the stops were inside normal intraday noise for a $200 stock with a 3% daily range.
  6. Fees were immaterial on paper ($0.97 of REG and CAT fees for the week) but the margin interest charged in late October (-$210.03) is a reminder that the account is running 4x buying power.

Suggested focus for week 2

  • Move to equal-notional or ATR-based sizing (for example 5% of equity per position, which at ~$6,300 is about $315) so every trade risks roughly the same amount.
  • Default to flat-by-close; if a position is carried overnight, it must carry a stop. The TEM hold had no stop until Friday morning.
  • Attach an OCO bracket to every entry but set the stop from the 14-day ATR (roughly 1.0 to 1.5 ATR) rather than a fixed percentage.
  • Log the signal that produced each entry (Depot Score, Momentum Pro, or discretionary) at order time so week 2's report can attribute P&L by strategy.
  • Keep deposits out of performance by recording them as they happen; this report subtracts them automatically from the equity curve.
  • Regenerate this report every Friday after the close from a fresh Alpaca pull using scripts/weekly_paper_report.py.
